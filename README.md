# Master Thesis: Expert Survey – Security Practices of Experts and Non-Experts

Thank you for considering participation in the expert survey for my master's thesis study.  
My name is Lisa Hoffmann, and I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Cybersecurity at Saarland University.  
For my thesis, I aim to replicate the study *“No One Can Hack My Mind: Comparing Expert and Non-Expert Security Practices”* originally conducted by [Ion et al.](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/soups2015/soups15-paper-ion.pdf), later replicated by [Busse et al.](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/soups2019-busse.pdf) in 2019, and by [Ortloff et al.](https://amortloff.github.io/papers/Ortloff2025_ReplicationNoOne.pdf) in 2025.

### Short Overview
This study examines differences and similarities in IT security practices between experts and non-experts, as well as how these practices are perceived by both groups.  
During the survey, you will be asked about your own security practices and online behavior, as well as your perspective on advice intended for non-tech-savvy users.
All data collected in this survey will remain completely anonymous.

### Compensation
Please note that, in line with the previous studies, no compensation will be provided for participation in this survey.

### Requirements
- Participants must be at least 18 years old in order to provide informed consent.  
- As the survey is administered exclusively in English, participants should have at least an intermediate level of English proficiency to meaningfully engage with the questions and provide reliable responses.  
- Participants in this expert survey are required to have at least one year of experience working or studying in IT security or a related field.

### Time Requirement 
The survey is expected to take approximately 20 minutes, though the exact duration may vary depending on the depth of your responses.
